# Simulation Results (revised)

All numbers below are generated by the named scripts from raw files in this
directory.  The pre-revision figures (`tier*_*.png`, `comparison_*.png`) are
kept for reference but their captions in the old report are superseded;
see `../ASSESSMENT.md` §3 for why.

## Gate: theorem-to-code tests
`python -m pytest tests` — 20 tests.  On an exactly representable plant the
Lyapunov identity holds to 1e-15 and the projection inequality at every sample
(`tests/test_theorem_to_code.py`).  The legacy centre-repulsion option (now
off) violates it in ~30% of samples and is kept only as a documented negative.

## Mechanism × geometry (`experiments/exp_bias_factorial.py` → `factorial/`)
Lorenz tracking, m=27, k_e=10, covering 3×3×3 grid, 5 seeds; mean steady-state ‖e‖:

| geometry | projection | σ-mod | e-mod |
|---|---|---|---|
| fixed grid (W) | 0.158 ± 0.007 | 0.733 ± 0.022 | 0.562 ± 0.031 |
| adaptive (W,c) | 0.147 ± 0.004 | 0.400 ± 0.005 | 0.233 ± 0.002 |
| adaptive (W,c,σ) | 0.112 ± 0.004 | 0.386 ± 0.006 | 0.239 ± 0.003 |

Projection lower than both leaky baselines in 5/5 seeds in every geometry;
adaptive centres and widths each give a smaller, consistent (5/5) improvement.
No run diverged; hard bounds held.

## Monte Carlo (`experiments/exp_monte_carlo_honest.py` → `monte_carlo_honest/`)
Van der Pol, 200 random initial conditions: 200/200 finite, 200/200 inside the
parameter sets at every step, 200/200 satisfy the a-posteriori ISS bound
(max ratio 0.979).  Steady-state max ‖e‖: median 0.057, 95th pct 0.094, max 0.108.
No a-priori threshold is drawn (the old 0.5 line was arbitrary).

## Oracle bound study (`experiments/exp_oracle_ruub.py` → `oracle_ruub/`)
Representable plant, 4 gains × 4 learning-rate scales × 3 seeds: RMS bound with
the measured disturbance holds 48/48 and scales as 1/k_e; the a-priori R_UUB
(26–261) is 900×–11 000× above the observed RMS error (0.009–0.09).

## Kernel-method baselines
* Complete saved-run table, nothing excluded: `comparison_complete_table.md`,
  `comparison_higher_dims_complete.png` (proposed ranks 3/5, 2/5, 3/5, 2/5 by
  error; fastest by 2–4 orders of magnitude).
* Fair re-run (n = 1, per-step prediction for every method, 9×3 covering
  inducing grid, 3 seeds; `experiments/run_fair_n1.py` then `plot_fair_n1.py`;
  `fair_n1/table.md`, `fair_n1/figure.png`), mean steady-state ‖e‖ ± 95% CI and
  median full online step:

  | method | mean SS ‖e‖ | step (ms) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Exact GP (≤500 pts, refit every 50 steps) | 0.059 ± 0.006 | 6.3 |
  | Sparse GP (fixed 9×3 grid) | 0.187 ± 0.021 | 1.35 |
  | Proposed | 0.198 ± 0.050 | 0.06 |
  | Deep GP | 0.309 ± 0.112 | 4.5 |
  | NTK | 0.470 ± 0.025 | 0.21 |

  Once the GP baselines predict at every step the exact GP is 3–4× more
  accurate than the proposed method (3/3 seeds); the proposed method matches
  the sparse GP and beats deep GP / NTK, at 20–100× lower per-step cost.  The
  old claim "statistically indistinguishable from the Exact GP" is withdrawn.
