\section{Numerical evidence}\label{sec:evidence}

Every number in this section is produced by the named script from raw files, and no table is edited by hand. The
scripts are in the directory \code{experiments/} of the accompanying repository and write their output to
subdirectories of \code{results/}; ``\code{script.py} $\to$ \code{dir/}'' below names a script and its output
directory or file.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \begin{sloppypar}The test files check the inequalities at every sample of simulated trajectories, or on
    sampled states: \code{tests/test_transport.py}, \code{tests/test_langevin.py}, \code{tests/test_averaging.py},
    \code{tests/test_excitation.py}, \code{tests/test_nonreciprocity.py}, \code{tests/test_periodic.py} and
    \code{tests/test_barycentric.py}.\end{sloppypar}
  \item They are run with \texttt{venv\textbackslash Scripts\textbackslash python -m pytest tests}. The current
    number of tests is given in the repository's \code{README.md}.
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The pure leak stops exactly where $W^2=\mathrm{Var}_\gamma$ (every particle at the barycentre of its cell;
    $V_{\mathrm{bar}}^2\le2.2\cdot10^{-25}$): an equal-mass centroidal configuration of the prior. Like Lloyd's
    algorithm it finds a local minimiser of the quantisation energy, not always the global one (compare the last two
    columns of Table~\ref{tab:evidence:barycentric}).
  \item With $m_0=m$ the same leak gives the exact decay of Theorem C(i): the maximal relative deviation of
    $W(\mu_t,\mu^0)$ from $(1-\sigma_m\,\mathrm{d}t)^kW_0$ over 500 Euler steps is $5.1\cdot10^{-16}$.
  \item In the closed loop, the bias of the barycentric leak is governed by $b_{\mathrm{bar}}$, the distance of the
    ideal from the centroids of the prior's cells: $W(\mu_T,\mu^*)=0.167$, $0.097$, $0.157$ against
    $b_{\mathrm{bar}}=0.168$, $0.097$, $0.159$, not by $b=W(\mu^*,\nu)=0.45$, $0.33$, $0.40$ (Theorem C$'$(ii)--(iii)).
    The labelled leak to the subset ends at $W(\mu_T,\mu^*)\approx W(\mu^*,\mathrm{subset})$. A rich prior whose
    centroidal quantisation is close to the ideal is a better anchor than a crude prior with the right number of
    atoms.
  \item Tests (\code{tests/test_barycentric.py}): the plan reduces to the assignment for $m_0=m$ and
    $W^2=V_{\mathrm{bar}}^2+\mathrm{Var}_\gamma$; the pure leak dissipates at every step with
    $D^+Z\le-\sigma_mV_{\mathrm{bar}}^2$ and ends at a Lloyd fixed point; $\sqrt{C_{\mathrm{bar}}}\le b+2\eta$ on 90
    configurations, with $C_{\mathrm{bar}}=b_{\mathrm{bar}}^2$ at small $\eta$; the Lyapunov inequality (iii) along a
    closed loop with a 24-atom prior.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Proposition D\texorpdfstring{$'''$}{'''}.9: measure-valued priors and the design of the window}

Script: \code{exp_prior_design.py} $\to$ \code{chaos/prior_design.md}.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Same instance and leak as Theorem D$'''$'s numerics ($\sigma_0=0.15\sigma_{\mathrm{crit}}=0.00707$),
    nearest-atom leak toward three prior atoms. Matching prior (exact representation): fit error $0$, $\kappa_R=0$,
    margin $\sigma_0=0.00707$ against the $\sigma$-modification's $0.00196$ (the theorem's relaxation time
    $1/\kappa$ $141$ against $511$ slow units; observed late-time e-folding of the coupled distance 48--84 against
    107--306); $\norm{S_k}=13.01$, $6.18$, $8.91$ against $8.38$, $4.66$, $8.32$; $\E D_\infty(m=64)$ $0.323$
    against $0.201$ ($1.61\times$); function-level floor $9.33\cdot10^{-4}$ against $4.29\cdot10^{-4}$
    ($2.18\times$).
  \item Misplaced priors: centres $+0.15$ / $+0.3$ / $+0.45$ / $+0.6$ $\to$ margins
    $+0.0055$ / $+0.0037$ / $+0.0017$ / $-0.0006$; amplitudes $\times0.5$ / $\times0.7$ / $\times1.2$ $\to$
    $+0.0074$ / $+0.0073$ / $-0.0317$; widths $\times0.7$ / $\times1.5$ / $\times2$ $\to$ $-0.0261$ / $+0.0058$ / $+0.0040$;
    the shrunk priors ($\times0.5$, $\times0.7$) have the largest margins and lower floors ($\E D_\infty$ $0.199$,
    $0.230$; function level $7.1\cdot10^{-4}$, $7.7\cdot10^{-4}$) at fit errors $0.09$, $0.06$.
  \item Admissible weights (splitting margin of $\Theta^*(w)$): $(7,5,4)/16$: $+0.0026$ / $-0.0054$ / $+0.0073$;
    $(9,4,3)/16$: $-0.0022$ / $-0.0058$ / $+0.0057$; $(10,3,3)/16$: $-0.0038$ / $-0.0042$ / $+0.0054$;
    $(0.5,0.3,0.2)$: $-0.0006$ / $-0.0065$ / $+0.0066$ ($\sigma$-modification / matching / amplitudes
    $\times0.7$): the shrunk prior keeps all four admissible (above $\sigma_0$ only at $(7,5,4)/16$; its
