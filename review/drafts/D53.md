**Lemma D‴.53a (the inner problem is a gradient flow of the fit).** (i) With w_i = s_i𝒲_{ρ_i}(ξ_i) the inner problem is

0}. (ii) In the continuum (⟨·⟩ the integral over ℝⁿ divided by ℓ) the single-kernel fit of Aφ(c*) has, for w ≠ 0, the unique critical point
(A, c*); an entrant started at w = 0⁺ with sign A converges to it. On a finite set of samples the same holds once the entrant's fit has
fallen below its value at infinity, E(s₁) < liminf_{|c|→∞}E (otherwise a kernel can leave the samples' window with a growing coefficient,
⟨φ(c)²⟩ → 0 there), and its critical point with w ≠ 0 is unique in that sublevel set. (iii) *(the coupled entry)* If before the entry the
