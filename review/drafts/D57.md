**Proposition D‴.57 (the transit's dead zone: a dead zone on the residual's energy stops the transit at the discrepancy point, but
freezes the last block before it has relaxed — an rms error ≈ s(4K/N + 2δ + δ²)^{1/2} against the oracle's s(2K/N)^{1/2}; a dead zone on
the formation of new kernels only stops it with the oracle's fit, and no kernel fits noise at any time — in the sliding limit; in the
closed loop under measurement noise neither gate keeps the formed kernels in the window).** In the setting of Proposition
D‴.56 — data y = f* + ξ on N samples, i.i.d. noise of variance s² per sample, the noiseless sliding path exact with simple entries
j₁, …, j_K, the K-th block's fit nondegenerate —, gate the free law's drive r = y − Φ(c)w by the residual's energy:

(a) the energy dead zone: every particle's drive multiplied by 1{⟨r²⟩ > (1 + δ)²s²} — the classical robust-adaptive-control dead zone
(Peterson–Narendra), on the mean square over the samples;
(b) the formation dead zone: only the particles not yet formed — |w_i| ≤ ϑ, with ϑ above the seeds' and the creep's coefficients
(O(ε^{3/2}), D‴.48) and below the features' fitted sizes — gated, the formed ones driven as before.

(i) *(the formation dead zone is the discrepancy principle)* In the sliding limit the path with (b) is the noisy sliding path stopped at
the first entry after which the relaxed residual's rms is ≤ (1 + δ)s: D‴.56(iv)'s discrepancy rule — at the K-th entry unless
|R_Kξ| > (1 + δ)sN^{1/2}, of probability P(χ²_{N−2K} > (1 + δ)²N) at first order (late), and unless the noise is comparable to the last
feature's fitted size (early). Stopped at the K-th entry its block keeps relaxing to the K-kernel least-squares fit of y: f̂ − f* =
(I − R_K)ξ + O(|ξ|²), the oracle's error s(2K/N)^{1/2} in rms, and no noise kernel forms at any later time — the window W of D‴.56(iii)
never closes.

(ii) *(the energy dead zone freezes the last block)* With (a), on the stop at the K-th entry — the event |r_K|² ≤ cs², c = (1 + δ)²N,
r_K = R_Kξ + O(s²) the K-th block's relaxed residual, |R_Kξ|²/s² ~ χ²_{N−2K} — the gate closes during the K-th block's relaxation, when
the residual's rms falls to (1 + δ)s. At first order the frozen residual is r_K + u, u = Φ(θ_K) − Φ(θ_c) the block's unrelaxed part —
in the fit's tangent space T, orthogonal to r_K —, so that

    |u|² = (1 + δ)²s²N − |r_K|²,

and f̂ − f* = (f̂_K − f*) − u: the oracle's fit displaced by the unrelaxed part. The relaxation reaches the fit along the block's slowest
mode, a direction e set by the noiseless block, so u = |u|e, |u| a function of R_Kξ alone, and the cross term ⟨f̂_K − f*, u⟩ =
|u|⟨P_Tξ, e⟩ has mean zero given the stop (P_Tξ and R_Kξ are independent): with m_c = E[χ²_{N−2K} | χ²_{N−2K} ≤ c] = (N − 2K)P(χ²_{N−2K+2}
≤ c)/P(χ²_{N−2K} ≤ c),

    E[|u|² | stop at K]/N = s²(c − m_c)/N,   E[|f̂ − f*|² | stop at K]/N = s²(2K + c − m_c)/N + o(s²)   (the oracle's: s²·2K/N),

→ s²(2δ + δ² + 2K/N) and s²(4K/N + 2δ + δ²) as the late stops' probability P(χ²_{N−2K} > c) → 0. For K = 3, N = 61, δ = 0.1 (P(late)
= 0.046, m_c = 53.83 against N − 2K = 55): |u|/√N ≈ 0.57s against the oracle's 0.31s, the rms error 2.08 times the oracle's, the excess
on the last block's slowest mode.

(iii) *(at a finite mass)* The loop at the mass ε with either gate follows these limits up to the stop (Theorem D‴.53 on y). After it
the energy dead zone's state stays where it stopped, and the formation dead zone's formed block relaxes to the fit while the unformed
particles stay at their seed-level coefficients: the residual's energy does not increase (the formed block's flow is the fit's gradient
flow, the data are fixed), so a closed gate stays closed.

(iv) *(the closed loop, numerical — a negative result)* In D‴.49's closed loop the data are not fixed: the regressor moves with the
plant, the noise enters the measured error, and the gate sees the error's filtered energy, whose floor near the fit is the high-passed
noise. The gates then do not reproduce (i)–(iii): the formed kernels themselves are carried out of the reference's range — where, as on noisy samples (D‴.56), the fit has no minimiser and a kernel leaving the range fits the noise at the edge — and a
gate on the formation of new kernels does not act on them; the energy dead zone, which also freezes the formed block, keeps more of it.
Only a leak (the mirror leak, at its bias) or a bound on the centres keeps the kernels in the window (D‴.39's dissipativity; D‴.56's box).

*Proof.* (i) Along the sliding path on y the relaxed residual's energy decreases at each entry (the block's fit is a minimiser over a
larger set) and is constant between entries; the gate on the unformed particles is open while the energy exceeds (1 + δ)²s²N/N, and the
formed block, ungated, relaxes. At first order (D‴.56(i), (iv)) the K-th relaxed residual is R_Kξ with |R_Kξ|²/s² ~ χ²_{N−2K}, and before
the K-th entry the residual contains the unfitted feature, of energy above the threshold when s is below its fitted size. Once the gate
closes the unformed duals stop, so no further entry occurs; the block's end is the K-kernel least-squares fit (D‴.56(iv)). (ii) During the
K-th relaxation the energy decreases monotonically (the fast flow is the fit's gradient flow, Lemma D‴.53a); on the stop at K its limit
|r_K|² is below (1 + δ)²s²N, so it crosses that level at a point θ(t_c) whose distance from the fit θ* is O(s), where r = y − Φ(θ*) +
Φ(θ*) − Φ(θ(t_c)) = R_Kξ + J(θ* − θ(t_c)) + O(s²), the second term in T; the energy identity gives |u|², and f̂ − f* = Φ(θ(t_c)) − f* =
(I − R_K)ξ − u. A gradient flow reaches a nondegenerate minimum tangent to its slowest eigenvector v₁ (generic starts): at the crossing
θ(t_c) − θ* = a₁e^{−λ₁t_c}v₁ + Σ_{i>1}a_ie^{−λ_it_c}v_i with a_i = O(1) (the entrant comes from its seed) and e^{−λ₁t_c} = O(s), so the
faster modes are O(s^{λ₂/λ₁}), relatively O(s^{λ₂/λ₁−1}); v₁, λ_i and the sign of a₁ are the noiseless block's up to O(s), so u = |u|e
with e ξ-independent at leading order. For Gaussian ξ the orthogonal projections P_Tξ and R_Kξ are independent, |u| and the event of the
stop are functions of R_Kξ, so the cross term's conditional mean is E[|u| | stop]E⟨P_Tξ, e⟩ = 0; E[|P_Tξ|²] = 2Ks² and E[χ²_k1{χ²_k ≤ c}]
= kP(χ²_{k+2} ≤ c) give the conditional mean squares. (iii) D‴.53's convergence holds for any data up to
the stopping time; with every drive gated the state is stationary, and with only the unformed ones gated the formed block's flow decreases
the energy (Lemma D‴.53a), so the gate's condition persists. ∎

*Status.* (i) at first order (D‴.56's); (ii) the identity at first order, the conditional mean squares for Gaussian noise under the
slowest-mode approach;
(iii) up to the stop, D‴.53; (iv) numerical, on one closed loop. Classical: the dead zone in robust adaptive control (Egardt; Peterson–Narendra; Ioannou–Sun), Morozov's
discrepancy principle and early stopping (Yao–Rosasco–Caponnetto; Raskutti–Wainwright–Yu), Bregman/inverse scale space iterations
stopped by the discrepancy principle (Burger–Osher et al.). New: for the paradigm's sparse kernel formation the dead zone is the
discrepancy principle, its classical form costs about twice the oracle's error by freezing the last block mid-relaxation (with the
error's exact first-order law), and gating only the formation of new kernels gives the oracle's fit with the noiseless support — on fixed
data; in the closed loop the formed kernels' escape from the window is the binding failure, and no gate on the drive's energy prevents
it.
