*Proof.* (i) Along the sliding path on y the relaxed residual's energy is non-increasing in the entries (each block's fast flow is the
fit's gradient flow, Lemma D‴.53a, started from the previous block's relaxed state with the entrant at its seed) and constant between
them; the gate on the unformed particles is open while the energy exceeds (1 + δ)²s²N, and the formed block, ungated, relaxes. At
first order (D‴.56(i), (iv)) the K-th relaxed residual is R_Kξ with |R_Kξ|²/s² ~ χ²_{N−2K}, and before the K-th entry the residual
contains the unfitted feature, of energy above the threshold when s is below its fitted size. Once the gate closes the unformed duals
stop, so no further entry occurs; the block's end is the K-kernel least-squares fit (D‴.56(iv)). (ii) During the K-th relaxation the
energy decreases monotonically; on the stop at K its limit |r_K|² is below (1 + δ)²s²N, so it crosses that level at a point θ(t_c)
whose distance from the fit θ* is O(s), where r = y − Φ(θ*) + Φ(θ*) − Φ(θ(t_c)) = R_Kξ + J(θ* − θ(t_c)) + O(s²), the second term in T;
the energy identity gives |u|², and f̂ − f* = Φ(θ(t_c)) − f* = (I − R_K)ξ − u. With θ(t_c) − θ* = −δw·e_last + (the others' and the
entrant's centre's lag), the others' lag O(overlap·δw) and the centre's measured negligible, u = J(θ* − θ(t_c)) = δw·P⊥φ_last + O(s²)
once the others' response — their best response to the entrant's amplitude, which the fast flow keeps them at — removes from φ_last its
projection on the rest of T. For Gaussian ξ the orthogonal projections P_Tξ and R_Kξ are independent; given the order kept, the last
entrant and P⊥φ_last are fixed and δw and the stop are functions of R_Kξ, so E[⟨P_Tξ, P⊥φ_last⟩δw | stop] = 0 up to the conditioning
on the order, whose probability tends to 1; E|P_Tξ|² = 2Ks² and E[χ²_k1{χ²_k ≤ c}] = kP(χ²_{k+2} ≤ c) give the conditional mean
squares; c − m_c = c − (N − 2K) + o(1) once P(χ²_{N−2K} ≤ c) → 1, and |P⊥φ_last|²/N tends to the continuum's ‖φ‖²/|Ω| on the samples'
window Ω. (iii) D‴.53's convergence holds for any data up to the stopping time; with every drive gated the state is stationary, and
with only the unformed ones gated the formed block's flow decreases the energy (Lemma D‴.53a), so the gate's condition persists. ∎
