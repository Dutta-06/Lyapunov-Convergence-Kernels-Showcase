**Theorem D‴.55 (exact recovery by the sliding transit: from small masses separated features are recovered exactly, each by its nearest
starting kernel, whatever the amplitudes; at a finite separation the outcome is the path's skeleton's — the inverse scale space with the
fitted features' atoms moved onto them — up to the blocks' nonlinearity).** In the setting of Proposition D‴.48(iii) in ℝⁿ (Proposition
D‴.54(i)) — Gaussian kernels of width σ, the paradigm's moving centres, equal masses ε (ρ = 1), started at the points of a finite set Γ of
spacing h — let the target be K features,

    f* = Σ_{k=1}^K A_kφ(·; c*_k),   A_k ≠ 0,   |c*_k − c*_l| ≥ Δ (k ≠ l),

e_k the point of Γ of largest correlation with feature k, ⟨φ(·; c*_k)φ(·; c_{e_k})⟩ — the nearest point in the continuum; assumed unique —,
E = {e_1, …, e_K}, ⟨·,·⟩ the samples' mean (or the continuum's integral), R = max_k|c*_k − c_{e_k}|, and

    the single-feature margin   m₀ := min_k (1 − max_{j≠e_k}⟨φ(·; c*_k)φ(·; c_j)⟩/⟨φ(·; c*_k)φ(·; c_{e_k})⟩)   (> 0 by uniqueness),
    the cross-talk              q := max_k Σ_{l≠k} G((Δ_{kl} − 2R)₊)/G(R),   G(d) = e^{−d²/(4σ²)}.

*The skeleton.* The inverse scale space on the starting kernels with each fitted feature's atom moved onto it: before any entry the residual
is r₀ = f*; once the kernels e_{m₁}, …, e_{m_k} have entered, r_k = f* − Π_kf*, Π_k the orthogonal projection onto T_k = span{φ(·;
c*_{m_i}), ∇_cφ(·; c*_{m_i}) : i ≤ k} (the fitted features' atoms and their tangent directions); between entries every inactive kernel's
dual grows at ⟨r_kφ(·; c_j)⟩ from its starting point, and the next entry is the first inactive dual to reach ±1. The skeleton is exact if
its K entries are the kernels of E, each for its own feature; its margin m_sk is 1 − max |u_j| over the entries and the kernels off E, or,
when a kernel off E enters, minus the unfitted features' kernels' least distance from their walls at that moment. It is K least-squares
projections and a recursion for piecewise linear duals — no flow.

(i) *(one feature)* For K = 1 in the continuum the path is exact with e₁: the single kernel's fast flow on the critical curve keeps w of A's
sign (w = 0 is invariant, and the entrant starts with A's sign) and converges to (A, c*) — its reduced energy ½ZA²(1 − G(c − c*)²) decreases
monotonically to the centre, which moves inward at the speed Gm|w||A|ZG(d)|d|/(2σ²) —, and every other kernel's dual ends at G(c_j −
c*)/G(c_{e₁} − c*) < 1. On samples ⟨φ_c, ∇_cφ_c⟩ ≠ 0 near the window's edge, and the entrant is the kernel of largest correlation, which
need not be the nearest there.

(ii) *(the skeleton is the path's first order)* Between the k-th and (k+1)-th entries the path's block sits at a stationary point of the fit
near (c*_{m_i}, A_{m_i})_{i≤k}, displaced by δ_k = O(the unfitted features' tails projected on the block's tangent directions, relative to
the fitted amplitudes); its residual is r_k + O(δ_k²) — the nearest point of the block's manifold to f* is f*'s projection on its tangent
space up to the manifold's curvature —, so the path's duals are the skeleton's up to O(δ²) accumulated over the phases, and the path enters
exactly E iff the skeleton does, outside a band |m_sk| = O(δ²). The fitted skeleton — each block at that stationary point itself (the
sign-constrained least-squares fit of the fitted features' kernels started at the features) in place of the projection — is the path, duals
and all, whenever each block's fast flow ends at the stationary point near its features: it has no band, only the flow's basins. The
amplitudes enter through the order of the entries and the phases' lengths; dropping the projections (Π_k = 0) and keeping one feature per
phase, the duals at the end are the amplitude-free certificate η(c_j) = Σ_k sign(A_k)⟨φ(·; c*_k)φ(·; c_j)⟩/⟨φ(·; c*_k)φ(·; c_{e_k})⟩ up to
O(q): the skeleton's zeroth order.

(iii) *(the separated regime)* There is Δ₀ depending on σ, h, R/σ, m₀ and n only such that for Δ ≥ Δ₀ the sliding path is exact with E for
all amplitudes: it enters exactly E, each kernel once, none leaves, each e_k slides onto its feature, and the path ends at the exact fit
(w_{e_k} = A_k, c_{e_k} = c*_k). With Proposition D‴.51 and Theorem D‴.53 the loop at the mass ε then forms exactly E once ε is small
against (μ/x_θ)² for the margins μ at the entries and below the creep's threshold — x_θ = ln(|A_k|/θ − 1)/κ grows with the amplitudes'
range, θ below min|A|, so the mass, unlike the path, depends on the amplitudes.

(iv) *(the certificate at a finite separation)* The skeleton's sign decides the path's outcome except at near ties and where a block's fit
moves beyond its linear regime (two same-sign features of comparable amplitude near 3σ apart), the fitted skeleton's on every target
computed (the blocks' flows ending at the stationary points near their features); the amplitude-free certificate decides only far from its
threshold, in a band whose width depends on the amplitudes — the same positions and signs are exact at one amplitude ratio and not at
another. In the continuum two same-sign features at distance D add at their midpoint 2G(D/2)/G(R) ≥ 1 once D² ≤ 16σ²(ln 2 + R²/(4σ²)) (≈
3.3σ at a midpoint on a starting point), so η fails there; it is not a bound on where the path fails.

(v) *(two features: the two ends of the amplitude ratio)* For K = 2 with the feature a fitted first, the second phase's rates are ⟨r₁φ_j⟩ =
A_b⟨(I − Π₁)φ(·; c*_b), φ_j⟩ — proportional to A_b —, so the second entry depends on the amplitudes only through the duals at the first,
u_j(τ₁) = (A_ag_{aj} + A_bg_{bj})/|A_ag_{ae_a} + A_bg_{be_a}| (g_{kj} = ⟨φ(·; c*_k)φ(·; c_j)⟩). As A_b/A_a → 0 the skeleton's outcome tends
to that of the single-feature profile u_j(τ₁) = sign(A_a)g_{aj}/g_{ae_a} followed by the screened phase — independent of the ratio —, and
likewise as A_a/A_b → 0 with the roles exchanged; between, the two features' tails add in the first phase's duals. The outcome is constant
at both ends of the ratio (the two ends' outcomes may differ) and changes only in a window about the ratio where the order of the entries
flips.

*Proof.* (i) Normalise Z = 1. For one feature and one kernel ⟨rφ_c⟩ = AG(d) − w and ⟨r∇_cφ_c⟩ = −AG(d)d/(2σ²) (d = c − c*; ⟨φ_c, ∇_cφ_c⟩ = 0
in the continuum): on the critical curve ẇ = W′M(AG(d) − w) and ḋ = −GmwAG(d)d/(2σ²); {w = 0} is invariant, so w keeps the seed's sign, |d|
decreases, and the flow ends at (A, c*); the entry's inner problem (D‴.51(ii)) is this flow in s. The other kernels' duals grow at AG(c_j −
c*) until τ₁ = 1/(|A|G(d)) and stop: u_j = sign(A)G(c_j − c*)/G(d). (ii) The block's stationary point solves min |f* − Φ(c)w|² near the
fitted features' exact fit, a nondegenerate minimiser of the fit of its own features (the block's kernels at distinct, separated centres);
f* = Σ_{fitted}A_mφ_m + f_u with f_u the unfitted features, and the implicit function theorem gives the stationary point at (c*, A) + O(|P
f_u|) with the residual (I − Π_k)f_u + O(|P f_u|²κ) (κ the manifold's curvature ∝ 1/|A_m|); the duals' increments are these rates over the
phases. Dropping Π_k, the phase in which feature m is the next to be fitted ends when e_m's dual reaches 1, after a time (1 +
O(q))/(|A_m|⟨φ_m, φ_{e_m}⟩) over which feature m feeds every dual at A_m⟨φ_m, φ_j⟩: the amplitude cancels, leaving sign(A_m)⟨φ_m, φ_j⟩/⟨φ_m,
φ_{e_m}⟩, and the other unfitted features' contributions over the same time are O(q). The fitted skeleton: between entries the path's block
is at a stationary point of its own fit (the sliding limit, D‴.48(iii)) and the inactive duals grow at its residual's correlations; if each
block's flow ends at the stationary point near its features, the fitted skeleton's residuals, hence its duals and entries, are the path's,
and no kernel of E exits (the fit keeps the features' signs, |w| near |A_m|). (iii) The features are fitted in the order of |A_k|⟨φ_k,
φ_{e_k}⟩ up to factors 1 + O(q), so a feature unfitted when another is fitted has |A_l| ≤ |A_m|(1 + O(q))/G(h√n/2): the blocks'
displacements and the screening are O(G(Δ − 2R)·Δ/σ) uniformly in the amplitudes. Each relaxation: on T = {|c_i − c*_{k(i)}| ≤ max(2R, σ/2),
sign(w_i) = sign(A_{k(i)})} the block's energy is the sum of the active kernels' one-feature energies plus couplings whose forces, relative
to the fitted amplitudes, are O(q); by (i) the uncoupled flow moves every centre inward at a speed bounded below on the boundary of T, so
for Δ large the flow cannot leave T, the entrant enters T at its starting point and grows before it moves, and inside T the block's critical
point is unique (the uncoupled one moved by O(q)) — the flow, a gradient flow of the fit (Lemma D‴.53a) with mobilities bounded above and
below on T, converges to it by Łojasiewicz's inequality. The duals off E end within O(q) of the single-feature values, which are ≤ 1 − m₀;
no exit, |w_i| ≥ |A_{k(i)}|(1 − O(q)); after the K-th entry the critical point in T is the exact fit, r = 0, and no kernel enters. Δ₀ is
where the O(q) terms fall below m₀. (v) In the skeleton's second phase r₁ = f* − Π₁f* = A_b(I − Π₁)φ(·; c*_b), since φ(·; c*_a) ∈ T₁; every
inactive kernel's time to its wall, (1 − sign(⟨r₁φ_j⟩)u_j(τ₁))/|⟨r₁φ_j⟩|, is then 1/|A_b| times a ratio-free quantity given u(τ₁), and
u_j(τ₁) = v_j/|v_{e_a}|, v_j = A_ag_{aj} + A_bg_{bj}, is continuous in the ratio with the stated limit; the first entrant tends to e_a. In
the fitted skeleton feature b's tail displaces the first block by O(|A_b/A_a|), with the same limit. (iv) The midpoint algebra: 2G(D/2) ≥
G(R) iff D² ≤ 16σ²(ln 2 + R²/(4σ²)). ∎
