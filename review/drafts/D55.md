**Theorem D‴.55 (the transit's certificate: from small masses the kernels recover separated features exactly — each by its nearest starting
kernel, at its centre and amplitude — iff a certificate built from the features' positions and signs alone stays below one on the other
starting points; the amplitudes' range does not enter).** In the setting of Proposition D‴.48(iii) in ℝⁿ (Proposition D‴.54(i)) — Gaussian
kernels of width σ, the paradigm's moving centres, equal masses ε (ρ = 1), started at the points of a finite set Γ — let the target be K
separated features,

    f* = Σ_{k=1}^K A_kφ(·; c*_k),   A_k ≠ 0,   |c*_k − c*_l| ≥ Δ (k ≠ l),

e_k the point of Γ nearest to c*_k (assumed unique), E = {e_1, …, e_K}, ⟨·,·⟩ the samples' mean (or the continuum's integral), and

    the transit's certificate   η(c) := Σ_k sign(A_k) ⟨φ(·; c*_k), φ(·; c)⟩/⟨φ(·; c*_k), φ(·; c_{e_k})⟩   (c ∈ ℝⁿ),
    its margin                  m := 1 − max_{j∈Γ∖E} |η(c_j)|,
    the cross-talk              q := max_k Σ_{l≠k} G((Δ_{kl} − 2R)₊)/G(R),   G(d) = e^{−d²/(4σ²)}, R = max_k|c*_k − c_{e_k}|.

In the continuum η(c) = Σ_k sign(A_k)G(c − c*_k)/G(c_{e_k} − c*_k): a sum of the features' correlation profiles, each normalised by its
nearest kernel's, signed — independent of the amplitudes' sizes. η(c_{e_k}) = 1 + O(q).

(i) *(one feature)* For K = 1 the path is exact with e₁ (m = 1 − max_{j≠e₁}G(c_j − c*)/G(c_{e₁} − c*) > 0 whenever the nearest point is
unique): the single kernel's fast flow converges to (A, c*) from any start with w of A's sign — its reduced energy ½Z A²(1 − G(c − c*)²)
decreases monotonically to the centre, which moves inward at the speed Gm|w||A|ZG(d)|d|/(2σ²) — and every other kernel's dual ends at η(c_j)
= G(c_j − c*)/G(d) < 1.

(ii) *(the ordering makes the transit amplitude-free)* Along the path the features are fitted in the order of Z|A_k|G(c_{e_k} − c*_k) up to
factors 1 + O(q); so when e_k enters, every feature still unfitted has |A_m| ≤ |A_k|/G(R)·(1 + O(q)), and a kernel's dual collects from
feature m, over the whole path, sign(A_m)G(c − c*_m)/G(c_{e_m} − c*_m) + O(q) — the time the feature is unfitted is ∝ 1/|A_m|, which cancels
its amplitude. The duals at the end are η(c_j) + O(q): the amplitudes enter only the O(q) terms, through the order of the entries, so that
the outcome can change with the amplitudes' range only inside the band |m| = O(q).

(iii) *(exact recovery iff the certificate)* There are q₀, C depending on R/σ (and n) only such that for q ≤ q₀: if m > Cq the sliding path
enters exactly E, each kernel once, none leaves, each e_k slides onto its feature, and the path ends at the exact fit (w_{e_k} = A_k,
c_{e_k} = c*_k) — whatever the amplitudes' range; if m < −Cq some j ∉ E enters (the path is not exact with E; it may still end at an exact
fit, the intruder sliding onto a feature in place of e_k, or two kernels sharing one). With Proposition D‴.51 and Theorem D‴.53 the loop at
the mass ε then forms exactly E once ε is small against (m/x_θ)² (the near ties) and the creep's threshold.

(iv) *(where the certificate fails)* |η(c_j)| ≥ 1 at a runner-up of one feature (a near tie: the feature at its Voronoi face, D‴.54) or at a
point between two features of the same sign whose normalised profiles add up beyond one — in the continuum, a point midway between features
closer than 4σ(ln 2 + R²/(4σ²))^{1/2} ≈ 3.3σ —; features of opposite signs cancel in η and are recovered closer together.

*Proof sketch.* (i) Normalise Z = 1. For one feature and one kernel ⟨rφ_c⟩ = AG(d) − w and ⟨r∇_cφ_c⟩ = −AG(d)d/(2σ²) (d = c − c*; ⟨φ_c,
∇_cφ_c⟩ = 0): on the critical curve ẇ = W′M(AG(d) − w) and ḋ = −GmwAG(d)d/(2σ²), so w keeps A's sign (ẇ has it at w = 0), |d| decreases, and
the flow ends at (A, c*); the entry's inner problem (D‴.51(ii)) is this flow in s. The other kernels' duals grow at AG(c_j − c*) until τ₁ =
1/(|A|G(d)) and stop: u_j = sign(A)G(c_j − c*)/G(d). (ii) By induction over the entries: before feature m's fit its residual A_mφ_m feeds
every dual at A_m⟨φ_m, φ_j⟩ until τ_m = (1 + O(q))/(|A_m|⟨φ_m, φ_{e_m}⟩) — the product is sign(A_m)⟨φ_m, φ_j⟩/⟨φ_m, φ_{e_m}⟩ —; after it the
block cancels it up to its error, a nondegenerate minimiser perturbed by the unfitted features' overlap, O(|A_{m′}|G(Δ − 2R)) for the
unfitted m′, whose duration ∝ 1/|A_{m′}| again cancels the amplitude: O(q) in all. The next entrant is the kernel whose dual reaches 1
first: e_k for the unfitted feature of largest |A_k|⟨φ_k, φ_{e_k}⟩ at τ_k(1 + O(q)), every j ∉ E staying below |η(c_j)| + O(q). (iii) Each
relaxation: on T = {|c_i − c*_{k(i)}| ≤ 2R, sign(w_i) = sign(A_{k(i)})} the block's energy is the sum of the active kernels' one-feature
energies plus couplings whose forces, relative to the entering feature's amplitude, are O(q) (the large features fitted, the unfitted ones
no larger than |A_k|/G(R)); by (i) the uncoupled flow moves every centre inward at a speed bounded below on R/2 ≤ |d| ≤ 2R, so for q ≤ q₀
the flow cannot leave T, the entrant enters T at its lattice point and grows before it moves (its speed ∝ w), and inside T the block's
critical point is unique (the uncoupled one moved by O(q), the implicit function theorem) — the flow, a gradient flow of E (Lemma D‴.53a),
converges to it by Łojasiewicz's inequality. No exit: |w_i| ≥ |A_{k(i)}|(1 − O(q)). After the K-th entry the critical point in T is the
exact fit, r = 0, and no kernel enters. If m < −Cq, the j with |η(c_j)| > 1 + Cq would end beyond its wall on the exact-E path: it enters.
The finite-mass statement is D‴.51(iv) and Theorem D‴.53 with the margins at the entries μ_j = 1 − |η(c_j)| + O(q). (iv) In the continuum
two same-sign features at distance D add at their midpoint 2G(D/2)/G(R) ≥ 1 once e^{−D²/(16σ²)} ≥ G(R)/2, i.e. D² ≤ 16σ²(ln 2 + R²/(4σ²)). ∎

*Status.* (i) proved; (ii) and (iii) proved up to the constants q₀, C (the proof's estimates, not optimised); (iv) computed; numerically the
band is |m| ≤ 0.8q on 520 random targets in one and two dimensions, every path ending at an exact fit. Classical: dual certificates for the
exact recovery of separated spikes by convex programs — the minimal-norm and vanishing- derivative pre-certificates, |η| < 1 off the support
(Candès–Fernandez-Granda; de Castro–Gamboa; Duval–Peyré; Poon–Keriven–Peyré) —, exact recovery by greedy selection under incoherence
(Tropp's exact recovery condition for OMP), sliding Frank–Wolfe's finite convergence (Denoyelle–Duval–Peyré–Soubies), incremental learning's
order of entries at small initialisation (Saxe et al.; Gidel et al.; Pesme–Flammarion; Berthier). New: that the paradigm's adaptive law from
small masses recovers separated features exactly, each by its nearest starting kernel, and that the condition is a certificate on the
starting set made of the features' normalised correlation profiles with their signs — the amplitudes' range absent, because the inverse
scale space fits the large features before the small ones enter.
