**Proposition D‴.50 (the rich transit under label noise: the loop's dual is a Langevin diffusion — the kernels' stationary law is the
Gibbs measure of the Bregman projection's dual, with an exact fluctuation–dissipation identity; the mean fit survives the noise; the
transit's noise is, to first order, the noise's projection on the active face).** In the setting of Proposition D‴.45 on a dictionary
(Φ the N × d design of full row rank, J = (m/2N)|Φw − y|², the particles on free curves Q_i = q_i > −c from α = 0, so η = Φᵀζ), let
the labels carry an isotropic white noise of intensity σ, read in Stratonovich's sense on the curves (the limit of noises with smooth
paths, D‴.45(iv)(a)):

    dζ = (y − Φw)dt/N + (σ/N)dW,   W a standard Brownian motion in ℝ^N.

The mirror update (D‴.45(iv)(b)) is this equation's Euler–Maruyama scheme — the unadjusted Langevin algorithm on F below —; the
paradigm's Euler update on (α, log β) is the Itô reading, which drifts the conserved Q and is not covered. Write T = σ²/(2N),
D = diag(dw_i/dη_i) (= m·relevance) and F(ζ) = Ψ*_Q(Φᵀζ) − yᵀζ, the objective of the Bregman projection's dual: its minimiser ζ* gives the
noiseless loop's limit w* = ∇Ψ*_Q(Φᵀζ*) (D‴.45(iii)).

(i) *(the Gibbs law and its exact identity)* ∇F(ζ) = Φw − y, so dζ = −∇F(ζ)dt/N + (2T/N)^{1/2}dW — an overdamped Langevin diffusion at
the temperature T. On free curves inf D_i > 0, so ∇²F = ΦDΦᵀ ⪰ (min_i inf D_i)ΦΦᵀ: F is strongly convex and the law of ζ converges to
π ∝ exp(−F/T) from every start, exponentially in W₂ — at a rate ≥ min_i inf D_i·λ_min(ΦΦᵀ)/N, which is small at small masses (on the rich
limit's optimal face the dual crosses in a time Λ²N²/σ², (iii)) —; the scheme samples π up to O(h) over practical horizons (its drift
grows exponentially, so it is not globally stable in the strict sense). Pathwise (1/t)∫₀ᵗ(y − Φw)ds = N(ζ(t) − ζ(0))/t − σW(t)/t. Hence
(a) E_π[Φw] = y at every noise level — the noise does not bias the fit (the exact mean fit of D‴.45(iv)(a)), and the time-averaged
coefficients fit the labels at the rate t^{−1/2}; (b) integration by parts, E_π[∇F∇Fᵀ] = T·E_π[∇²F], gives the exact identity

    Cov_π(Φw) = T·ΦE_π[D]Φᵀ   at every noise level;

(c) at fixed masses, as T → 0, E_π[w] = w* + O(T) and Cov_π(w) = T·D^{1/2}PD^{1/2} + O(T²) (D at w*, P the orthogonal projector onto
range(D^{1/2}Φᵀ)) — Laplace's expansion at the non-degenerate minimiser, valid while T ≪ λ_min(ΦD(w*)Φᵀ)·δ² (δ the dual's distance to the
region where D changes), which is not uniform in the masses: in the rich regime with fewer active kernels than samples the optimal face's
directions have a stiffness that vanishes with ε, and (c) fails there while (b) holds.

(ii) *(fluctuation–dissipation on a support)* Let S be a support on which Φ_S has full column rank. By (i)(b),

    Cov_π(w_S) = T·E_π[D_S] + R_off,   R_off = Φ_S⁺(TΦ_offE_π[D_off]Φ_offᵀ − Φ_offCov_π(w_off)Φ_offᵀ)Φ_S⁺ᵀ − C_× − C_×ᵀ,
    C_× = Cov_π(w_S, w_off)(Φ_S⁺Φ_off)ᵀ,

so where the off-support coefficients and mobilities are negligible — the rich regime after the transit — the coefficients on the support
fluctuate without correlation, whatever the design's correlations, each with the variance T·E_π[dw_i/dη_i]; this is ≈ T·dw_i/dη_i at
w_BP when T·dw_i/dη_i ≪ w_i² (D varies on the scale |w_i|: a coefficient whose fluctuations reach its own size is skewed toward zero and
its mobility's mean departs from its value at w_BP). On the critical curve dw/dη ≈ 4Gc|w|/a_max for saturated amplitudes (sd ∝ |w|^{1/2})
and ≈ 12Gc²α⁴/(ma_max⁴) ∝ |w|^{4/3} for small ones (the depth-three unit).

(iii) *(the rich limit; formal)* For masses εb, as ε → 0 at fixed σ, the dual's law in ν = ζ/Λ(ε) concentrates on the optimal face of
weighted basis pursuit's dual, max{yᵀν : |φ_iᵀν| ≤ ρ_i}; inside it the off-support coefficients vanish, so by (i)(a) E_π[w_S] → w_BP,S
when Φ_S has full column rank: the stationary mean is weighted basis pursuit's solution (the limit ε → 0 at fixed σ, not uniform in σ).
The face is crossed only on the time Λ²N²/σ², and the coefficients do not see where on it the dual is, except at the face's boundary,
where an off-support coefficient reaches O(T^{3/2}) while its curve's small-α branch holds (β_j ~ γmT ≪ 2c), O(1) beyond.

(iv) *(the noisy transit)* In τ = t/Λ the noise enters with the intensity κ = σ/(NΛ^{1/2}) ∝ σε^{1/4}: dν = (y − Φw)dτ/N + κdW_τ. The
noiseless limit is the inverse scale space flow (D‴.48(i)), the normal Skorokhod problem of the dual polytope {|φ_iᵀν| ≤ ρ_i} driven by
yτ/N; on a path of entries only the noise adds to first order the deviation δν(τ) = κP_{T(τ)}W(τ), P_T the orthogonal projector onto the
tangent space ker Φ_Aᵀ of the active face A(τ) — the active kernels absorb the noise's normal part, an entering kernel the deviation
accumulated along its normal, the inactive dual coordinates diffuse along the face (an exit adds a first-order jump not included here).
The response fails within windows around the entries of the larger of two widths: the noise's entry shift O(κ/|u̇_j|), within which the
two runs have different active sets, and the entering kernel's finite-ε absorption time O(N/(Λ·D_j|P_{T⁻}φ_j|²)) ∝ ε^{1/2}, within which
its normal mode, whose mobility D_j is still small, has not yet absorbed the accumulated deviation — an error linear in the noise, the
same fraction of κ at every σ. Outside the windows the response holds up to the active block's Ornstein–Uhlenbeck component, of mean square
κ²Σ_i1/(2Λλ_i) (λ_i the block's normal relaxation rates), relative size O((Λλ)^{−1/2}). The entries keep their order while κ is small
against the gaps between them; the transit converges to the inverse scale space at the rate σε^{1/4} (half the noiseless ε^{1/2}) — the
price of parsimony, a transit time ∝ ε^{−1/2}, buys the averaging of the noise.

*Proof.* (i) ∇_ζΨ*_Q(Φᵀζ) = Φ∇Ψ*_Q(η) = Φw. The generator −(1/N)∇F·∇ + (T/N)Δ is symmetric in L²(π). On a free curve dw/dη =
(β/m)(Gβa′² + γa²) is bounded below by a positive constant (β ≥ q + c > 0 bounds the first term away from zero where a′ is, the second
where a is), so ∇²F ⪰ δΦΦᵀ with δ > 0: F is strongly convex, π a probability measure, and the Bakry–Émery criterion (equivalently the
synchronous coupling of two solutions) gives W₂(law_t, π) ≤ e^{−δλ_min(ΦΦᵀ)t/N}W₂(law_0, π) from every start. Integrating the equation gives
the pathwise identity; E_π[∇F] = 0 by integration by parts gives (a); E_π[∂_iF∂_jF] = T·E_π[∂_{ij}F] by integrating ∂_i(∂_jF·e^{−F/T})
(the boundary terms vanish by the strong convexity) gives (b), with ∇F = Φw − y, E_π[∇F] = 0 and ∇²F = ΦDΦᵀ. (c) Laplace's method at the
non-degenerate minimiser: Cov_π(ζ) = T(ΦDΦᵀ)⁻¹ + O(T²) and δw = DΦᵀδζ, so Cov_π(w) = T·DΦᵀ(ΦDΦᵀ)⁻¹ΦD = T·D^{1/2}PD^{1/2}; the
expansion needs the Gaussian's width (T/λ)^{1/2} along each eigendirection of ∇²F small against the scale on which ∇²F varies there.
(ii) Write Φw = Φ_Sw_S + Φ_offw_off in (b) and apply Φ_S⁺ on both sides (Φ_S⁺Φ_S = I_S). The asymptotes: on the critical curve
β = 2c sinh²(α/a_max) and dw/dη = (β/m)(Gβa′² + γa²); saturated, βa′ → 2c and a → a_max, so dw/dη → γa_max²β/m = 4Gc|w|/a_max (γa_max = 4Gc/a_max
on the paradigm's instance); small, β ≈ 2cα²/a_max², a ≈ α, so dw/dη ≈ (2cα⁴/(ma_max²))(γ + 2Gc/a_max²) = 12Gc²α⁴/(ma_max⁴) while
w ≈ 2cα³/(ma_max²). (iii) Formal: π in ν is ∝ exp(−(Λ/T)G_ε(ν)) with G_ε(ν) = F(Λν)/Λ, which epi-converges to −yᵀν + χ_P(ν) (D‴.48(i)'s
potentials plus a linear term), so by the Laplace principle at the temperature T/Λ → 0 the law concentrates on the argmin, the optimal
face; inside it the off-support constraints are strict and the off-support coefficients O(ε^{3/2}) (D‴.45(iii)'s core tail), and
E_π[Φw] = y with Φ_S of full column rank gives the mean. On the face the potential is flat (yᵀν constant), so the dual diffuses with the
coefficient σ²/(2N²) across a face of size O(Λ). Beyond an off-support wall the Gibbs weight is exp(−ψ*_j/T), ψ*_j = 2c sinh²(α_j/a_max)/(γm):
the dual penetrates where ψ*_j ~ T, i.e. β_j ~ γmT, where w_j = β_ja(α_j)/m = O(T^{3/2}) on the small-α branch. (iv) Formal: within a
phase of fixed active set the noise's component normal to the face is restored by the active block's linearised dual, at the rates Λλ_i
in τ — an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck component of mean square κ²Σ_i1/(2Λλ_i) remains —, the tangential one is a martingale, dδν = κP_TdW; an entry
happens when u_j = φ_jᵀν reaches ρ_j, shifted by −δu_j/(du_j/dτ) under D‴.48(i)'s simple, transversal events, and from then on both runs
hold φ_jᵀν = ρ_j: the deviation loses its component along P_{T⁻}φ_j, δν(τ_e⁺) = P_{T⁺}δν(τ_e⁻) (range P_{T⁺} = range P_{T⁻} ⊖ span P_{T⁻}φ_j).
The tangent spaces are nested along a path of entries, P_{T(τ)}P_{T(s)} = P_{T(τ)} for s ≤ τ, so the increments compose to κP_{T(τ)}W(τ).
The loss of the component along P_{T⁻}φ_j is not instantaneous at a finite ε: the entrant's normal mode relaxes at the rate
Λ·D_j|P_{T⁻}φ_j|²/N in τ, slow while its coefficient, hence D_j, is small — the absorption window; between τ_e and the shifted entry the
two runs have different active sets and their duals differ at O(κ) — the shift window. (A leave — a coefficient reaching zero — enlarges
the tangent space, and its shift adds a first-order jump (P_{T⁺} − P_{T⁻})yδτ_l/N, not included above.) ∎
