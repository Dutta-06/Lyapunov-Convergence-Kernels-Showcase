**Proposition D‴.56 (the transit under measurement noise: the sliding path's linear response decides the support; the first kernel to
fit noise enters after a window ∝ 1/s; stopped in that window the fit is the oracle's).** In the setting of Theorem D‴.55 — Gaussian
kernels of width σ started at the points of Γ, equal masses, the samples' mean ⟨·⟩ over N samples — let the target f* have an exact
sliding path with simple entries — the kernels j₁, …, j_K at τ₁ < … < τ_K, each block S_i relaxed to a nondegenerate stationary point
of the fit, no exits (D‴.55: the nearest kernels E when m > Cq) — and let the data be y = f* + ξ, ξ a perturbation of the samples.
Write, along the noiseless path, J_i and H_i for block i's Jacobian on the samples in its coefficients and centres and the fit's Hessian
there (the residual's curvature included), R_i = I − J_iH_i⁻¹J_iᵀ/N, v_j^{(i)} = ⟨r_iφ_j⟩ the inactive kernels' rates, u⁰_j(τ_k) their
duals at the entries.

(i) *(the linear response)* For |ξ| small the path on y has the same entries in the same order, at τ_k + T_kξ + O(|ξ|²), and the duals
of the kernels off the path at the entries are u⁰_j(τ_k) + U_{k,j}ξ + O(|ξ|²); the rows T_k, U_{k,j} follow by recursion over the
entries — block i's residual moves by R_iξ, the rates by ⟨R_iξ, φ_j⟩, and the entry conditions give
δτ_{i+1} = δτ_i − (δu_{j_{i+1}}(τ_i) + (τ_{i+1} − τ_i)⟨R_iξ, φ_{j_{i+1}}⟩)/v^{(i)}_{j_{i+1}},
δu_j(τ_{i+1}) = δu_j(τ_i) + (δτ_{i+1} − δτ_i)v^{(i)}_j + (τ_{i+1} − τ_i)⟨R_iξ, φ_j⟩ (`path_response`).
(ii) *(the support)* The path on y enters exactly the noiseless path's kernels first iff, for every entry k ≤ K and kernel j off them,
|u⁰_j(τ_k) + U_{k,j}ξ| < 1, up to O(|ξ|²) — the duals are piecewise linear in τ, so their extremes lie at the entries. For i.i.d.
noise of variance s² per sample the U_{k,j}ξ are jointly Gaussian with variances s²|U_{k,j}|² (|·| the Euclidean norm on the samples),
so 1 − P(exact) ≤ Σ_{k,j}2Φ̄((1 − |u⁰_j(τ_k)|)/(s|U_{k,j}|)) + O(s²): the noise's scale against the path's own margins, which are the
certificate's up to O(q) (D‴.55).
(iii) *(the window)* After the K-th entry the block sits at the K-kernel least-squares fit of y; its residual is R_Kξ + O(|ξ|²), R_K the
projection off the fit's tangent space T (dim T = 2K in one dimension), and the next entry — the first kernel to fit noise — comes after
W = min_{j off}(1 − sign(v_j)u_j(τ_K))/|v_j|, v_j = ⟨R_Kξ, φ_j⟩: exactly, given the K-th block, and at first order with
u_j(τ_K) = u⁰_j(τ_K) + U_{K,j}ξ. As s → 0, s·W converges in law to min_j(1 − sign(Z_j)u⁰_j(τ_K))/|Z_j|, Z ~ N(0, Σ⊥),
Σ⊥_{jl} = ⟨φ_j, R_Kφ_l⟩/N: the window is ∝ 1/s, set by the path's final margins and the noise's part off T; its relative error is O(s).
(iv) *(stopping; the oracle)* Stopped in (τ_K, τ_K + W) the fit is the K-kernel least-squares fit of y with free centres:
f̂ − f* = (I − R_K)ξ + O(|ξ|²), the noise's projection on T, of rms ≈ s(2K/N)^{1/2} — the error of the oracle that knows the support,
with no shrinkage of the amplitudes. Two rules stop there: the discrepancy principle (stop at the first entry after which the relaxed
residual's rms is below (1 + δ)s), when s is below the last feature's fitted size; and, without s, the largest relative gap
(τ_{k+1} − τ_k)/τ_k, since W/τ_K ∝ 1/s exceeds the features' own gaps for small s.

*Proof.* (i) Each block's stationary point is nondegenerate (H_i ≻ 0 on its own coordinates), so by the implicit function theorem it
moves smoothly with y: δθ_i = H_i⁻¹J_iᵀξ/N, δr_i = ξ − J_iδθ_i = R_iξ. Between entries the inactive duals are linear in τ with the rates
⟨r_iφ_j⟩; an entry is transversal (v^{(i)}_{j_{i+1}} ≠ 0), so its time moves smoothly, and differentiating sign(v)u_{j_{i+1}}(τ_{i+1}) = 1
and u_j(τ_{i+1}) = u_j(τ_i) + (τ_{i+1} − τ_i)v_j gives the recursion; for |ξ| small no other kernel's dual reaches its wall first (the
margins at the entries are positive), so the order is kept. (ii) The path enters exactly the noiseless kernels iff no other dual reaches
±1 before τ_K; the duals are piecewise linear, so it suffices at the entries; (i) and the union bound over the Gaussian margins. (iii)
After the K-th entry the block is at its stationary point on y — the least-squares fit (polished; D‴.53's block) — and the residual is
constant until the next entry, so the inactive duals grow linearly at ⟨r_Kφ_j⟩ and the next one reaches its wall at the stated W; R_K is
the projection off T because the residual vanishes at the noiseless fit (H_K = J_KᵀJ_K/N); v is linear in ξ and u_j(τ_K) → u⁰_j(τ_K),
hence the law of sW. (iv) The stopped block is the K-kernel fit, δθ_K = H_K⁻¹J_Kᵀξ/N, so f̂ − f* = J_Kδθ_K + O(|ξ|²) = (I − R_K)ξ +
O(|ξ|²), whose mean square is s²·rank(I − R_K)/N = s²·2K/N in expectation. ∎

*Status.* (i) proved (first order; the recursion exact at that order); (ii) sharp at first order — no constant C, unlike D‴.55(iii),
whose O(q) the response's u⁰ contains —, the Gaussian bound a union bound; (iii) exact given the K-th block, first order otherwise, with
the limit law of sW; (iv) the oracle at first order, the rules' success numerical. Classical: the inverse scale space and linearised Bregman
iterations as regularisation paths, with a stopping time at which the support is recovered exactly and the estimate is the oracle's —
sign consistency under the irrepresentable condition (Osher–Ruan–Xiong–Yao–Yin) —; the LASSO's support recovery under noise by the
primal–dual witness (Wainwright); the BLASSO's support stability at low noise with λ ∝ noise (Duval–Peyré); the discrepancy principle
(Morozov) and early stopping as regularisation (Yao–Rosasco–Caponnetto); Bregman iterations' semi-convergence (Burger–Resmerita–He).
New: the off-grid, moving-centre version for the paradigm's law from small masses — the exact first-order response of the sliding path
(its entry times and duals) to the data, which makes the support criterion sharp at first order and gives the window's 1/s law with its
limit law, and the stopped fit's oracle error with unbiased amplitudes and centres. At a finite mass ε the loop follows this noisy path
(Theorem D‴.53 applies to any data), in the time t = Λ(ε)τ.
