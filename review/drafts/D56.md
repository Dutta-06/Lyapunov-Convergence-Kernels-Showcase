**Proposition D‴.56 (the transit under measurement noise: the sliding path's first-order response decides the order of the entries
and the support, outside a band O(|ξ|²) about the walls; the first kernel to fit noise enters after a window ∝ 1/s; stopped in that
window the fit is the oracle's at first order).** In the setting of Theorem D‴.55 — Gaussian kernels of width σ started at the points of
Γ, equal masses, the samples' mean ⟨·⟩ over N samples — let the target f* have an exact sliding path with simple entries — the kernels
j₁, …, j_K at τ₁ < … < τ_K, each block S_i relaxed to a nondegenerate stationary point of the fit, no exits (D‴.55: the starting kernels
of largest correlation E for separated features; at a finite separation the fitted skeleton's outcome) — and let the data be
y = f* + ξ, ξ a perturbation of the samples (in (ii)–(iv) i.i.d. Gaussian, of variance s² per sample).
Write, along the noiseless path, J_i and H_i for block i's Jacobian on the samples in its coefficients and centres and the fit's Hessian
there (the residual's curvature included), R_i = I − J_iH_i⁻¹J_iᵀ/N (R₀ = I: before the first entry the residual is the data),
v_j^{(i)} = ⟨r_iφ_j⟩ the inactive kernels' rates, u⁰_j(τ_k) their duals at the entries.

(i) *(the linear response)* There is a ball |ξ| ≤ r₀ on which the path on y has the same entries in the same order, at τ_k + T_kξ +
O(|ξ|²), and the duals of the kernels not yet entered at the entries are u⁰_j(τ_k) + U_{k,j}ξ + O(|ξ|²), the remainders at most C|ξ|²;
the rows T_k, U_{k,j} follow by recursion over the entries from τ₀ = 0, δτ₀ = 0, δu(0) = 0 — block i's residual moves by R_iξ, the rates
by ⟨R_iξ, φ_j⟩, and the entry conditions give
δτ_{i+1} = δτ_i − (δu_{j_{i+1}}(τ_i) + (τ_{i+1} − τ_i)⟨R_iξ, φ_{j_{i+1}}⟩)/v^{(i)}_{j_{i+1}},
δu_j(τ_{i+1}) = δu_j(τ_i) + (δτ_{i+1} − δτ_i)v^{(i)}_j + (τ_{i+1} − τ_i)⟨R_iξ, φ_j⟩ (`path_response`).
At first order the radius is set by the margins of the kernels not yet entered — those off the path and the path's later entrants:
r₀ ≥ r₁ = min_{k,j}(1 − |u⁰_j(τ_k)|)/|U_{k,j}| up to O(r₁²) (|·| the Euclidean norm on the samples), and by the blocks' nondegeneracy
(λ_min(H_i) > 0, lost at |ξ| of order λ_min(H_i)/‖∂_yH_i‖). A near tie of consecutive entries is a small margin of the later entrant —
at τ_k the next entrant's margin is (τ_{k+1} − τ_k)|v^{(k)}_{j_{k+1}}| —, so the radius is set by the smallest gap between consecutive
entries as much as by the margins off the path.
(ii) *(the order and the support)* Let crit(ξ) = max over the entries k ≤ K and the kernels j not yet entered at τ_k of
|u⁰_j(τ_k) + U_{k,j}ξ| (the order criterion; over the kernels off the path alone, the support criterion). For |ξ| ≤ r₀: if
crit(ξ) < 1 − C|ξ|², the path on y enters the noiseless path's kernels, in the noiseless order, before any other; if crit(ξ) > 1 + C|ξ|²,
it does not — a dual reaches its wall out of turn: a later entrant (the order swaps) or a kernel off the path. The duals are piecewise
linear in τ, so their extremes lie at the entries. A swapped order with the support kept is outside the first order's description (the
linearisation runs along the noiseless order). For i.i.d. Gaussian noise U_{k,j}ξ ~ N(0, s²|U_{k,j}|²): P(crit < 1) is the first-order
probability that the order is kept and Σ_{k,j}2Φ̄((1 − |u⁰_j(τ_k)|)/(s|U_{k,j}|)) a union bound on its complement; the true probability
differs from P(crit < 1) by at most P(|crit − 1| ≤ C|ξ|²) + P(|ξ| > r₀) — of order s where failures are frequent (the band C|ξ|² ≈ CNs²
against crit's spread ∝ s), exponentially small in 1/s² where the margins are fixed.
(iii) *(the window)* After the K-th entry the block sits at the K-kernel least-squares fit of y; its residual is R_Kξ + O(|ξ|²), R_K the
projection off the fit's tangent space T (dim T = 2K in one dimension), and the next entry — the first kernel to fit noise — comes after
W = min_{j off}(1 − sign(v_j)u_j(τ_K))/|v_j|, v_j = ⟨R_Kξ, φ_j⟩: exactly, given the K-th block, and at first order with
u_j(τ_K) = u⁰_j(τ_K) + U_{K,j}ξ. As s → 0, s·W converges in law to min_j(1 − sign(Z_j)u⁰_j(τ_K))/|Z_j|, Z ~ N(0, Σ⊥),
Σ⊥_{jl} = ⟨φ_j, R_Kφ_l⟩/N: the window is ∝ 1/s, set by the path's final margins and the noise's part off T; inside the first order's
regime (crit < 1 − C|ξ|²) its relative error is O(s).
(iv) *(stopping; the oracle)* Stopped in (τ_K, τ_K + W) the fit is the K-kernel least-squares fit of y with free centres:
f̂ − f* = (I − R_K)ξ + O(|ξ|²), the noise's projection on T, of mean square s²·2K/N — the error of the oracle that knows the support, its
amplitudes and centres unbiased at first order. The discrepancy principle — stop at the first entry after which the relaxed residual's
rms is below (1 + δ)s, a stopping time — stops at the K-th entry unless |R_Kξ|² > (1 + δ)²s²N, of probability P(χ²_{N−2K} > (1 + δ)²N) at
first order (late), provided s is below the last feature's fitted size (beyond, it stops early). The largest relative gap
(τ_{k+1} − τ_k)/τ_k is not a stopping time but a selection over a fixed horizon of entries, which needs the path past the stop: it selects
the K-th entry iff W/τ_K exceeds every other relative gap of the horizon — the features' own (k < K), of the order of their amplitude
ratios (≈ |A_k|/|A_{k+1}| − 1 for separated features entering by amplitude, so growing with the amplitude range and with K), and the later
noise kernels' (k > K), not bounded uniformly. As W/τ_K ∝ 1/s, for a fixed target and horizon it selects the K-th entry with a probability
tending to one as s → 0, but not uniformly in the amplitude range, K or the horizon. Past τ_K + W the fit on y can have no minimiser: a
kernel leaving the samples' window fits the noise at the edge with a coefficient growing without bound, so the path there is defined only
with the centres confined (here to the window ± 2σ; a relaxation that carries a centre out stops the path).

*Proof.* (i) Each block's stationary point is nondegenerate (H_i ≻ 0 on its own coordinates), so by the implicit function theorem it
moves smoothly with y: δθ_i = H_i⁻¹J_iᵀξ/N, δr_i = ξ − J_iδθ_i = R_iξ (before the first entry the residual is y itself, R₀ = I). Between
entries the inactive duals are linear in τ with the rates ⟨r_iφ_j⟩; an entry is transversal (v^{(i)}_{j_{i+1}} ≠ 0), so its time moves
smoothly, and differentiating sign(v)u_{j_{i+1}}(τ_{i+1}) = 1 and u_j(τ_{i+1}) = u_j(τ_i) + (τ_{i+1} − τ_i)v_j gives the recursion. The
entry times and duals are C² in ξ while no other dual reaches its wall first and the blocks stay nondegenerate — a closed ball about 0 —,
which gives the remainder's bound C|ξ|² on it; at first order no dual of a kernel not yet entered reaches its wall while every
|u⁰_j(τ_k) + U_{k,j}ξ| < 1, which holds for |ξ| < r₁ by Cauchy–Schwarz; the later entrant's margin at τ_k is the next entry's gap times
its rate. (ii) The path enters the noiseless kernels in order iff, at each entry, no kernel not yet entered has reached ±1; the duals are
piecewise linear, so it suffices at the entries; the true duals are within C|ξ|² of the first-order ones on the ball, which gives both
implications; the probabilities follow, the band's from crit's density at 1. (iii) After the K-th entry the block is at its stationary
point on y — the least-squares fit (polished; D‴.53's block) — and the residual is constant until the next entry, so the inactive duals
grow linearly at ⟨r_Kφ_j⟩ and the next one reaches its wall at the stated W; R_K is the projection off T because the residual vanishes at
the noiseless fit (H_K = J_KᵀJ_K/N); v is linear in ξ and u_j(τ_K) → u⁰_j(τ_K), hence the law of sW. (iv) The stopped block is the
K-kernel fit, δθ_K = H_K⁻¹J_Kᵀξ/N, so f̂ − f* = J_Kδθ_K + O(|ξ|²) = (I − R_K)ξ + O(|ξ|²), whose mean square is s²·rank(I − R_K)/N =
s²·2K/N; the relaxed residuals' energies fall at each entry and the K-th is |R_Kξ|² + O(|ξ|³) with |R_Kξ|²/s² ~ χ²_{N−2K}; the gap rule's
statement is a comparison of the horizon's relative gaps, W/τ_K ∝ 1/s against gaps that are O(1) in law. A kernel at the window's edge
moving outward keeps a growing coefficient fitting the noise at the last samples: the fit's infimum is approached at infinity. ∎

*Status.* (i) proved (first order; the remainder's bound C|ξ|² holds on a ball whose radius is at least r₁ at first order, neither C nor
r₀ computed); (ii) proved two-sided outside the band of width C|ξ|² about the walls, its probability at first order, the union bound
valid; (iii) exact given the K-th block, first order otherwise, with the limit law of sW; (iv) the oracle at first order, the discrepancy
rule's stop at first order, the largest gap's selection numerical. The largest-gap rule presumes features of the kernels' width, whose own
relative gaps are O(1) and below the window's: a feature wider than the kernels is formed by several entries whose relative gaps are
themselves O(1) (D‴.58), which the rule cannot tell from a noise kernel's. Classical: the inverse scale space and linearised Bregman
iterations as regularisation paths, with a stopping time ∝ 1/σ before which no false positive enters and at which the estimate is the
oracle's — Osher–Ruan–Xiong–Yao–Yin (sign consistency under the irrepresentable condition) —; early stopping of small-initialisation
