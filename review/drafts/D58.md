**Proposition D‴.58 (the transit under width mismatch: a feature wider than the kernels is formed as a sequence of Gaussian quadratures; the
error falls by t = (σ* − σ)/(σ* + σ) per kernel).** Kernels φ_σ(x − c) = exp(−(x − c)²/(2σ²)) on ℝ, a feature f* = Aφ_{σ*}(· − c*) wider
than them, σ* > σ — the kernel smoothed by its mixing law: f* = A(σ*/σ)·φ_σ * N(c*, τ²), τ² = σ*² − σ². Write r = ln(σ*/σ),
t = tanh(r/2) = (σ* − σ)/(σ* + σ) and λ = tanh r = τ²/(σ² + σ*²); (z_k, w_k)_{k ≤ K} the K-point Gauss–Hermite rule of N(0, 1),
D_{n,K} = E He_n(√2Z) − Σ_k w_k He_n(√2z_k) (He_n the probabilists' Hermite polynomials); ‖·‖ the norm of L²(ℝ).

(i) *(the half-squeeze rule; the exact error)* The K-kernel sum f_K = Σ_k a_kφ_σ(· − c* − y_k) with the nodes and amplitudes
y_k = (2σ² sinh r)^{1/2}z_k = τ(σ/σ*)^{1/2}z_k,  a_k = A(σ*/σ)^{1/2}w_k exp(y_k²/(2σ(σ + σ*)))
— the Gauss–Hermite rule of the reweighted mixing law N(0, τ²σ/σ*), positive amplitudes — has the error
‖f* − f_K‖² = ‖f*‖²(1 − t²)^{1/2} Σ_{n ≥ 2K} (tⁿ/n!) D_{n,K}²,
every term n < 2K (and every odd n) vanishing, D_{2K,K} = 2^K K!. Hence (1 − t²)^{1/4}(4^K K!²/(2K)!)^{1/2} t^K ≤ ‖f* − f_K‖/‖f*‖
≤ C_ϑ(t/ϑ)^K for every ϑ ∈ (t, 1): the error falls exactly at the rate t per kernel, lim_K (‖f* − f_K‖/‖f*‖)^{1/K} = t, its first term
(1 − t²)^{1/4}(πK)^{1/4}t^K(1 + O(1/K)).
(ii) *(moment matching is slower)* The Gauss–Hermite rule of the mixing law itself (nodes τz_k, amplitudes A(σ*/σ)w_k — matching the
moments of N(0, τ²)) falls at λ = tanh r per kernel; t/λ = 1/(1 + sech r) ∈ (1/2, 1): the half-squeeze rule gains a factor up to 2 per
kernel (2^K for narrow mismatch).
(iii) *(the transit forms it, by insertion from the flanks, up to an order K*)* The optimal K-kernel fit has the rate t with the constant
of (i) to within (1 − δ_t), δ_t ≈ 0.05%, 0.4–0.5%, 2–3% at τ/σ = 1/2, 1, 2 (K ≤ 10), its nodes those of the half-squeeze rule to
0.1–5%; after it the residual's correlation with a kernel at c, C(c) = ⟨r_K, φ_σ(· − c)⟩, is nonnegative with double zeros at the K
nodes (stationarity in the amplitudes and centres) and symmetric peaks on the flanks, 2.0–2.6σ beyond the outermost node at K = 1 and
moving out with K — so every inactive kernel's dual grows and the next to reach its wall is a flank kernel. The sliding path (Theorem
D‴.53) enters the kernels one at a time from alternating flanks, and the block — the previous optimum and the flank entrant — relaxes
to the optimal (K + 1)-kernel fit, for K + 1 ≤ K*(τ/σ); beyond, the block relaxes to a non-optimal stationary point and the path wanders
through exits and re-entries, reaching a later optimum at times — K* = 5, 6, 9, 11 at τ/σ = 1/2, 3/4, 1, 3/2 and ≥ 12 at 2 (at 1/4 at
least 4, the next optimum below the double-precision floor), the starting grid's spacing 0.83σ. K* grows with τ/σ but is not set by the
flank peak's distance alone (the stall after a peak at 2.94σ at τ/σ = 3/2, beyond peaks at 3.18σ and 3.34σ at 3/4 and 1/2): the
transit's quadrature is the optimal one while the mixing law is wide enough for the flank entrant to rearrange the block, and
saturates near e_{K*} otherwise — the narrow mismatch, where moving widths (D‴.48(v)) take the feature's width with one kernel.
(iv) *(under noise)* With noise of level s on N samples the discrepancy rule (the residual's rms within (1 + δ)s) stops the path at
K(s), the first K with e_K²‖f*‖² ≤ s²((1 + δ)² − 1 + 2K/N) for (i)'s errors e_K, and its error is ≈ (e_K² + 2Ks²/(N‖f*‖²))^{1/2}‖f*‖:
the oracle's s(2K/N)^{1/2} with K(s) ≈ log(‖f*‖/s)/log(1/t) kernels — a wrong width costs a factor K(s)^{1/2}.
(v) *(narrower features)* For σ* < σ the half-squeeze frame needs imaginary nodes (the mixing law's variance is negative): no real rule,
the super-resolution regime (D‴.52).
