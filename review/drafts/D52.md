**Proposition D‴.52 (close features: the transit counts two features a distance Δ apart after a delay ∝ Δ⁻² — a number-detection
statistic at the optimal order — and its two-kernel block places them along modes stiff as Δ² and Δ⁶, the support-recovery order).**
In the setting of Proposition D‴.48(iii) (the sliding transit of Gaussian kernels of width σ on a dictionary, equal masses), let
f* = A₁φ(c₁) + A₂φ(c₂), Δ = c₂ − c₁ > 0, A = A₁ + A₂; ⟨·⟩ the samples' mean.

(i) *(the second entry)* If A ≠ 0, let c̄ = (A₁c₁ + A₂c₂)/A, V = A₁A₂Δ²/A and T₃ = A₁A₂(A₁ − A₂)Δ³/A² (the pair's moments about its
centroid), so that f* = Aφ(c̄) + ½V∂²_cφ(c̄) + ⅙T₃∂³_cφ(c̄) + O(|A₁A₂|Δ⁴/|A|); the expansion parameter is ϑ = |A₁ − A₂|Δ/(|A|σ). The first
entrant j₁ maximises |⟨f*φ_j⟩| (τ₁ = 1/|⟨f*φ_{j₁}⟩|), and its relaxation leaves the residual r₁ = ½Vψ(1 + O(ϑ)),
ψ = ∂²_cφ(c̄) − (⟨∂²_cφ(c̄)φ(c̄)⟩/⟨φ(c̄)²⟩)φ(c̄) — in the continuum ⟨ψ(c̄)φ(c̄ + d)⟩ = G(d)d²/(4σ⁴) ≥ 0, G ∝ e^{−d²/(4σ²)} the kernels'
autocorrelation. The inactive duals, u_j(τ₁) = ⟨f*φ_j⟩/|⟨f*φ_{j₁}⟩| in units of ρ, then move at v_j = ½V⟨ψφ_j⟩: toward the wall of A's
sign for a same-sign pair (A₁A₂ > 0), away from it for an opposite-sign pair, so the second entry comes at
    Δτ₂ = min_j (s_j − u_j)/v_j · (1 + O(ϑ)) ∝ Δ⁻²,   s_j = sign v_j,
— for a same-sign pair at the wall of A's sign (in general the first entry's runner-up), for an opposite-sign pair at the other wall (a
kernel on the pair's flank, with the minority's sign), the margin then 2 − μ instead of μ. If A = 0 (a dipole, A₂ = −A₁),
f* = −A₁Δ∂_cφ(c_m) + O(Δ³) (c_m the midpoint): the transit is the derivative shape's −A₁∂_cφ(c_m) slowed by 1/Δ — τ₁, Δτ₂ ∝ Δ⁻¹, the same
kernels. In every case the path's end is the exact two-kernel fit.

(ii) *(the block)* At the exact fit the Gauss–Newton Hessian JᵀJ of ½⟨(f* − Φ(c)w)²⟩ in (w, c) has two eigenvalues of order 1 and
    λ_sep = 4Δ²⟨(P⊥e₂)²⟩·w₁²w₂²/(w₁² + w₂²)·(1 + O(Δ)),   λ_split = (Δ⁶/8)⟨(P⊥e₃)²⟩·(1 + O(Δ)),
e_n = ∂ⁿ_cφ/n! at the pair and P⊥ the projection orthogonal to the lower e_m — the split's stiffness independent of the amplitudes. The
separation direction (0, 0, 1/w₁, −1/w₂) moves the centres apart and leaves Jv = −2Δe₂ + O(Δ²); the split direction
(1, −1, Δ/(2w₁), Δ/(2w₂)) moves weight from one kernel to the other with the centres following, cancels the residual's orders 0, 1, 2
and leaves Jv = (Δ³/2)e₃ + O(Δ⁴) (the confluent Vandermonde structure: the moment map (w₁, w₂, c₁, c₂) ↦ (M₀, …, M₃) has determinant

interval's length). The fast flow on the critical curve is, in (w, c), the gradient flow of the fit with the mobilities
μ_k = w′(α_k)·2Gc tanh²(α_k/a) on the amplitudes (≈ 4Gc|w_k|/a on the exponential branch: small) and Gm on the centres; it relaxes the

(iii) *(counting under noise)* With labels y = f* + ξ, ξ i.i.d. N(0, s²) on the N samples, the duals' rates after the first fit gain

resolution limit and Liu–Ammari's (σ/m)^{1/(2n−2)} at n = 2.

(iv) *(placing under noise)* The block's fit after the second entry has position errors ∝ s/Δ² and amplitude-split errors ∝ s/Δ³ (the

cluster of two (Batenkov–Goldman–Yomdin: SRF^{2ℓ−2}, SRF^{2ℓ−1}); the features are resolved when s ≪ Δ³, the support-recovery order
