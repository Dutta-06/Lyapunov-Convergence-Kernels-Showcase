**Proposition D‴.60 (measurement noise and the formed kernels: the adaptation's oscillator, the exact second order as Floquet sums, and
ejection — a drift on the slow modes, then a jump).** D‴.49's closed loop — ẋ = f(x) + u, u = ẋ_m − k_eẽ − f̂(x̃), ẽ = x̃ − x_m, x̃ = x + n,
the adaptation's regressor evaluated at x_m — let the formed kernels sit at the exact fit (each feature's kernel at its centre with its

(i) *(the linear response: an oscillator)* To first order in s the loop is its linearisation along the orbit at the fit. With K(t) =
Σ_i[μ_iφ_i² + v_iw_i(∂_cφ_i)²] at x_m(t), the fit's perturbation at the reference, y = Σ_i(φ_iδw_i + w_i∂_cφ_iδc_i), obeys —
quasi-statically in the orbit —

    y″ + k_eẏ + K(t)y = K(t)(ṅ − f̂′n),   δw_i = (μ_iφ_i/K)y,   δc_i = (v_i∂_cφ_i/K)y,

a damped oscillator whose frequency √K(t) sweeps along the orbit; a tone near the top of the sweep is amplified. Under the paradigm's metric
the centres' term dominates K; while the orbit passes c_i ± σ, each kernel's own frequency peaks at √K_i = (Gm)^{1/2}|w_i|/(σe^{1/2}) (the
largest (∂_cφ_i)², 1/(σ²e), up to the small μ_iφ_i²), so √K_max ∝ (Gm)^{1/2}, and the response peaks about 10% below √K_i (the sweep and the
damping k_e). Keeping √K_max below a band [ω_lo, ω_hi] of the noise — m ≲ ω_lo²/(G max_t Σ_iw_i²(∂_cφ_i)²) — reduces the resonance, without
removing it: the oscillator's half-power width is k_e, and the band's response over its roll-off alone (K = 0) falls only from 1.50 at m =

(ii) *(the second order: period means, Floquet sums)* Under the noise sν and −sν the loop's period means split into an even part, s²⟨θ₂⟩ +
O(s⁴), and an odd part, s⟨θ₁⟩ + O(s³), on any finite horizon, ⟨θ_k⟩ the period means of the loop expanded about the fit along the orbit (the
second order driven by the first through the plant's and the control's curvature, the control's slope moved by the jitter, the blur
½θ₁ᵀ∇²_θf̂θ₁, and the regressors' and mobilities' correlation with the drive — the sources of D‴.31(iv)(c) and D‴.33(i)). The linearised
loop is T-periodic (T = 2π/ω_ref) and exponentially stable: its monodromy has three slow multipliers — the formed kernels' coefficient
modes, 0.99400, 0.99245, 0.99237 at m = 12 (time constants τ = 166, 132, 131 periods) — and the others below 0.21. A forcing at the
frequency Ω adds over the period j the increment Re(e^{iΩjT}G) (G its response over one period from rest), so on a mode of multiplier ρ the
period means follow m_{j+1} = ρm_j + e^{iΩjT}δ and

    |m_j| = |δ||ρ^j − e^{iΩjT}|/|ρ − e^{iΩT}| ≤ |δ|(1 + ρ^j)/|1 − ρe^{iΩT}|:

bounded at every Ω, alternating at ΩT ∈ π + 2πℤ, and at commensurability (ΩT ∈ 2πℤ) an offset |δ|/(1 − ρ) ≈ |δ|τ/T reached with τ — the
classical response of a periodic system near a Floquet multiplier. The first order's period means are exactly such Floquet sums over the
tones ω_k, their increments falling steeply with ω. The second order's forcing is the products of the first order's tones: a constant part
(the tone averages ½Re(AB̄)) and parts at 2ω_k and ω_j ± ω_k; ⟨θ₂⟩ is the sum of their Floquet sums. The constant part's — an offset on the
slow coefficient modes reached with their τ, not a drift without bound — is the averaged loop's (the mean state with the first order's
complex envelopes, exact along the orbit), so the averaged loop's error is the dropped parts' Floquet sums, Σ_Ω|δ_Ω||ρ^j − e^{iΩjT}|/|ρ −
e^{iΩT}|, their increments numerical: large where some 2ω_k or ω_j ± ω_k is commensurate and its increment is not small (13.1 = 65.5ω_ref:
2ω = 131ω_ref), small where it alternates (13.05, 13.15: 2ω = 130.5ω_ref, 131.5ω_ref) or its increment is small (29.3 = 146.5ω_ref).

(iii) *(ejection; numerical)* A formed kernel leaves its feature by an ejection: its coefficient drifts up on the slow modes while its
centre stays at the feature, by about (ii)'s even part at its saturation, s²⟨w₂⟩, and then it jumps out of the orbit's range within a
period. At the threshold the leaves come after about one slow-mode time constant — a build-up on the slow modes, not a memoryless first
passage —, earlier at higher levels. Single tones drift further, their centre too, but at the levels tried never jump; tone pairs and bands
do — a pair one ω_ref apart from s = 0.04, D‴.49's own pair (its tones 18.0019ω_ref apart, its draw's phases) at s = 0.02 but not at
0.04–0.06. The jump level falls smoothly with the centres' mobility Gmw — the bound on m is no threshold — and rises with k_e; moving the
band above √K_max raises it, mostly through the band's roll-off and the rest through resonance. Under the Wasserstein–Fisher–Rao metric the
jumps come at levels an order of magnitude higher.
