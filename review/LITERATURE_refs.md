# Reference list from the literature audit (89 entries; 79 dropped as unsourced)

★ [1] R. M. Sanner, J.-J. E. Slotine (1992). *Gaussian networks for direct adaptive control*. IEEE Trans. Neural Networks 3(6):837-863. https://doi.org/10.1109/72.165588  
  Role: Claim A baseline: fixed-centre/width Gaussian RBF adaptive controller with Lyapunov-derived weight law (the law Claim A re-reads as a particle W2 flow); no measure/transport view.
★ [2] F. L. Lewis, A. Yesildirek, K. Liu (1996). *Multilayer neural-net robot controller with guaranteed tracking performance*. IEEE Trans. Neural Networks 7(2):388-399. https://doi.org/10.1109/72.485674

★ [3] P. A. Ioannou, P. V. Kokotovic (1984). *Instability analysis and improvement of robustness of adaptive control*. Automatica 20(5):583-594 (URL is a secondary Springer chapter citing it). https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/BFb0007553
