## Verdict

Lemma E.0 and the revised Theorem E are consistent end to end: every formula matches excitation.py symbol for symbol,
all eleven cited test names exist verbatim and assert exactly the tolerances the prose quotes, the test count 61 is
right for HEAD, and the Remark E.1 table (954, 17.6, slopes −1.97/−1.56, 4.7e-2, 2.9e-5) is converged and quoted
correctly. Five things need fixing. (1) Theorem I(iv) as committed is wrong: "r_μ = 0 ρ-a.e. ⇒ A = 2K" drops the curl
term, and the test that "checks" it uses f ≡ f̂_μ on all of ℝ², i.e. a stronger hypothesis than the theorem states (M5;
the parallel continuation has already corrected the statement in the working tree). (2) Both PARADIGM.md and README.md
cite a "round 5" of review/REVIEW_LOG.md that does not exist (M1). (3) RESULTS claims the nonlinear envelope is checked
"over one e-fold"; the test's horizon is 8000 s = 0.30 e-folds (M2). (4) The slow-adaptation ratios 6.8·10⁵ and 8.4·10¹⁰
are quadrature artefacts: the PE level α is under-resolved by 21–27 %, and converged values give 4.2·10⁵ and 5.6·10¹⁰
(M4); the "factor of about 2" attributed to the optimised ε is mostly Young-step and p_hi slack (m1). (5) Stale status
text: README open problem 3 still lists what LITERATURE says Theorem I settles (M3), README's H.7 row, PARADIGM §0
line 59 and two file lists were not updated. The manuscript sources are out of step in eight files, listed at the end.
