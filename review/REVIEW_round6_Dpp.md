## VERDICT

**The theorem holds as stated; the reading around it does not, in one major and several minor places.**

- *Proof.* Correct. Every step attacked in the brief checks out: the bracket decomposition and κ_e = k_e − L_f − L_x, the
  second-bracket contraction (leak without G, ‖ē‖ ≤ E_X because x̄ ∈ X), the Metzler/cooperative comparison with Dini
  derivatives and u(0) = 0, the Hurwitz criterion, ∫₀^∞e^{As}ds = −A⁻¹ and its explicit form, E ζ_s ≤ K_Φ m^{−1/2} for every
  s, exchangeability at fixed t, the W₁ triangle inequality. One inequality is loose by a factor 2 (DPP-1: the first
  bracket is ≤ L_b′‖δ‖, not 2L_b′‖δ‖), and the second-moment hypothesis of the W₁ statement is redundant (DPP-9). The
  differential inequality itself was confirmed pathwise on m-vs-M couplings with τ > 0 (ratio u/v ≤ 0.07 on the particle
  channel, also with the sharp factor 1).
- *Reading.* (i) **Major (DPP-3):** the experiment's σ = 0.02 run is *not* "spread" and *not* in the "learning regime". For
  this instance Proposition K's critical leak at the (unique) collapsed critical point — the dead kernel ϑ̄ = ϑ⁰, a = 0 — is
  σ₀_crit = 0.0291, i.e. σ_K = 0.00117 in leak-gain units, 17× below 0.02; a one-system sweep shows the particle spread
  decaying exponentially at rate σ − 0.0012 at every leak σ ∈ [0.02, 0.4] (half-life 37 s at σ = 0.02); the 80 s horizon is
  1.6 e-folds of a collapse to the trivial kernel, and the learned function never exceeds 12% of f at any tested leak. The
  Numerics paragraph, Remark D″.1's third bullet, the §7 row and §8 branch 3 assert an empirical learning-regime
  uniformity that the data do not contain. (ii) The "collapsed/trivial" characterisation (Status, Remark bullet 1, §0,
  README) is τ = 0-specific, while the theorem is for every τ ≥ 0: at τ > 0 the certified regime is the dither cloud
  N(ϑ⁰, (τ/σ)G) and the coupled distance is non-zero (10⁻³ against a bound of 0.2) — the theorem has non-trivial content
  there (DPP-2). (iii) The high-gain asymptotics in Remark bullet 2 drop the dominant O(1/k_e) term and tacitly need
  e(0) = 0 (DPP-4, DPP-5); the strong-convexity claim needs the a-priori E_X (DPP-6). (iv) The experimental "certified"
  label rests on an a-posteriori sample E_X; with the a-priori tube bound the text invokes (Theorem D(b)) the σ = 4.66 run
  is not certified (DPP-7), and the sampled Lipschitz constants under-estimate the exact suprema by up to 29% (DPP-8).
- No counterexample exists where the hypotheses hold and the conclusion fails — the proof is sound and the pathwise
  comparison inequality was verified numerically with a wide margin.
