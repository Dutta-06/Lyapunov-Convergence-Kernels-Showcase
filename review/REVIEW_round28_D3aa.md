## Verdicts

**The closed form and the two forms: correct.**
- ℓ = 2Σlog(W_k − W_min,k²/W_k) + const holds exactly (the constant is the same to 10⁻⁸ down to δ = 0.0025). c_k and the plug-in's g′_k identity are right.
- The masses form with Â fixed is a gradient: c_k depends on W_k alone, so it is a gradient even with the floor, though then of a bounded potential, not of ℓ. So the gradient-flow statement J − κ_F(τ/2)ℓ is right where the floor is inactive. The code's docstring ("neither is a gradient") contradicts it.
- "Best conditioned" is wrong (D3aa-3).

**(i): correct; the remainder is O(γ²τ) where stated.**
- `limit_drift`'s F₁ part equals the left-kernel projection to ≤ 5·10⁻¹¹ and (TᵀgT)⁻¹∇_WΦ to ≤ 3·10⁻⁷. That formula is the finite-γ form of the correction's drift, and the text does not give it.
- At W = 1/3, P·F_c/γτ deviates from the slow limit by 26γ, 34γ and 35γ (γ = 10⁻², 10⁻³, 10⁻⁴); the uncorrected drift deviates by about 28γ. So the remainder/(γ²τ) is 2.44, 2.23, 2.03 at γ = 1, 2, 3·10⁻⁴: O(γ²τ), with coefficient about 2.5.
- At W_1 = 0.27 it is 158, growing about like δ⁻³. So "outside the layers" is the right validity region.
- The over-compensation starts at γ ≈ 2·10⁻³, not 2·10⁻² (D3aa-6).

**(ii): correct as a slow-limit statement.**
- Strict concavity (f″ < 0 ⟺ W⁴ + 4cW² − c² > 0), the interior unique maximiser W♯ = (0.37652, 0.29876, 0.32472) and Shahshahani convergence all check.
- The slow-limit Jacobian at W♯, (κ_F − 1)γτ·(−5.8 … −7.3), matches B's −(5.7–6.8)γτ. The spread ∝ √γ checks (0.040, 0.014, 0.0046).
- The τ̂ statement is trivially right. Its numerical illustration mixes the mean and the rms of e (D3aa-8). With the rms, equipartition holds to 1%: p_eff = 8.92–9.04 with the correction, 8.06 without.

**(iii): the derivation is right; the implemented correction does not realise it.**
- The α-drift inside the layer is −9.89, −9.83, −9.67·10⁻⁵ at δ = 0.002, 0.003, 0.005, against the predicted −1.000·10⁻⁴.
- The derivation through the projection (dα/ds = G_α∂_αΦ on M) is cleaner than the sketch's quasi-equilibrium, whose "+O(a′_k)" needs λ to be read as the other classes' mean.
- The density e^{−2κ_Fα/a_max}, the exponent δ^{κ_F−1} and the finite-τ condition (0.003√κ_F) all re-derive.
- But below δ_cap = 0.00126 the 0.01W floor kills the confinement (D3aa-2).
- Part C is evaluated where the instance's τ violates the stated condition (D3aa-7).

**(iv): wrong to the order that matters at the instance.**
- κ⁻¹b_c is the formula's first order. The pinned mean has a fluctuation term of the same order: the leak's curvature acting on C. It is 30–70% of the shift at κ_F = 0 and larger than the shift at κ_F = 1.
- That term explains Part G's paired excess, to 0.3–0.9 s.e. The text calls the excess unexplained (D3aa-1).
- `kappa()` is correct (7·10⁻⁷).

**(v): the mechanism and Part F's evidence are as described.**
- The escapes are in seeds 401 and 413 in all four modes, from the same records (1.13·10⁵, 1.27·10⁵).
- The masses' ranges: 0.314–0.348 (none), 0.313–0.349 (masses), 0.211–0.461 (plug-in, only in the two escaping seeds, where all three amplitudes then saturate: the cascade), 0.304–0.366 (tracked).
- The fit errors are 0.0427/0.0439/0.0427/0.0426.
- The tracked variant is described correctly: the code averages the signed W_k a_k; Â = (1.382, −0.994, 1.096) and (1.391, −1.099, 1.164); Ŵ_1 → W_1.

**Numerics and reproducibility.**

## Lesson

A stationary mean has to be predicted to the order at which the fluctuations act. When the restoring force (here the leak's projected stiffness, which varies on the scale of the distance to the saturation) is curved, ½∂²L[C]/κ is of the same order τ/ε as the drift balance κ⁻¹b. A paired design, which cancels the noise, then resolves exactly this term, and it should be predicted, not reported as an unexplained 4–10% excess. Two related habits would have caught the other main issues. First, a limit statement should be stated and tested for the object the code implements, cap included: a floor that keeps the simulation finite also bounds the barrier, and normalisability is lost below it. Second, a name should be checked by computing the quantity it names. "Best-conditioned" took one eigenvalue computation to refute, "no excursion into the saturation" one histogram of \|α\| at κ_F = 1, and "the dither's level read back" one choice between ⟨e⟩² and ⟨e²⟩.
