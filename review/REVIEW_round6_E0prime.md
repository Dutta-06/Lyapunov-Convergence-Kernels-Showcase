## VERDICT: holds with fixes

The lemma is correct and its certificate is confirmed everywhere I looked. Part (a): the stated ẇ agrees with the chain
rule to 5·10⁻¹⁴ and with 4th-order finite differences of w = e − e₂(ζ, t) along DOP853 trajectories to 10⁻¹⁰, at
εΨ_M up to 3 (so every order up to ε⁶ is exercised). Part (b): Ṗ₁ = 2(M − M̄), Ṡ = F, ⟨F⟩ = 0 (‖⟨F⟩‖/sup‖F‖ ≤ 4·10⁻¹⁵),
the identity P₁M + MP₁ = P₁M̄ + M̄P₁ + ½d(P₁²)/dt (5·10⁻¹⁰, FD), the order-ε² coefficient vanishes to 10⁻¹³, and
d/dt[½ζᵀHζ] = −εζᵀM̄ζ + R_ζ + ζᵀHΨᵀw with exactly the stated R_ζ (10⁻¹²). Part (c): every per-direction bound holds
with the stated constant except one — the ε⁶ coefficient of c₂, Ψ_M⁵√ρ, is dimensionally wrong and is violated by the
factor Ψ_M (E0P-1); the correct Ψ_M⁶√ρ is attained with equality on the sinusoid. Part (d): Young step, β = 2c₁²/δ,
(A) ⇔ 2β²c₂²/(4δε) ≤ βk/4, the coefficient −β(k/2 − d_w) ≤ −βk/4 under (B), γ, and C (the (1+L)² conversion is a
valid operator-norm bound) are all right. The certificate sup_{t,x} V̇/V ≤ −γ passes on full grids of 1024–2048 times,
at random off-grid times, at random off-grid states, along trajectories, and in (k, δ) scans: 416 admissible pairs over
five regressors (sinusoid, two random multitones, a hand-made bursty ill-conditioned Ψ with κ(M̄) = 3.3·10³, the Theorem E
ideal with κ = 7.8·10³), 0 failures, min eig H ≥ 0.9455. No counterexample. The code implements the lemma exactly
(constants to 10⁻⁸, the residual being the FD Ψ̈; H, N to 10⁻¹³; factor 2 right; FFT antiderivative to 10⁻¹²).

Two asymptotic statements are false as written and should be fixed: the guaranteed rate is (1 − δ)(1 − εp₁ + O(k⁻²))λ_min/k,
not (1 − δ − O(k⁻²))λ_min/k (E0P-2), and k₀ ≈ (4ρρ_DD/δ²)^{1/4} holds only when (A) binds — on the sinusoid and the
multitone (C) binds and the formula is 2.2× and 15× too low (E0P-3). The claim about Theorem E's steps 4–5 in the
version I was given was an overclaim by the factor g_V ≈ β (E0P-4); the author has since reworded it and written
Corollary E.0′.2, whose proof is right but whose test fails.
